    About

    Mechanics, with a mind.

    I studied mechanical engineering with a strong foundation in mechanics, machine design, kinematics, dynamics and control systems, supported by CAD/FEA and simulation. This foundation has shaped how I approach robotics — understanding not just how a machine moves, but how its mechanical design, dynamics and control work together.

    Through internships and hands-on projects, I have worked across mechanical design, robotics, drone systems, vehicle testing, computer vision and machine learning. I enjoy building systems that connect mechanics, sensing and intelligent control — from autonomous drones and self-balancing robots to robotic mechanisms and vision-based systems.
